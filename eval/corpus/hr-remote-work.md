# Northwind Supply — Remote Work Guidelines

## Eligibility

Office-based employees who have passed probation may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Warehouse, delivery and front-desk roles are not eligible, because their work requires physical presence. Fully remote arrangements need approval from the department head and HR.

## Core hours

Remote employees must be reachable on Slack and by phone during core hours, 10:00 to 16:00 Tbilisi time. Outside core hours, schedules are flexible as long as the weekly hours in the employment contract are met. Remote days must be marked in the team calendar by Friday of the previous week.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop, headset and monitor for home use. Employees may claim a one-time home-office allowance of 300 GEL for a desk or chair; the claim is submitted through the expense system with a receipt. Company equipment remains company property and must be returned within 5 working days after employment ends.

## Working from abroad

Working remotely from another country is allowed for at most 20 working days per year and must be approved in advance by HR, because of tax and data-protection rules. Access to the finance and payroll systems from outside Georgia is blocked.

## Security when working remotely

Public Wi-Fi may only be used together with the company VPN. Screens must be locked when stepping away, and confidential documents must not be printed at home. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported immediately, as described in the IT security policy.

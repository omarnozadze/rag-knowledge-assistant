# Northwind Supply — Customer Returns Procedure

## General rules

Customers may return most products within 14 days of delivery, provided the product is unused, in its original packaging and accompanied by the receipt or order number. The refund is issued to the original payment method within 5 working days after the warehouse confirms the inspection. Shipping costs for returns are paid by the customer unless the product is defective.

## Return steps for staff

1. Check the order in the ERP system and confirm the delivery date.
2. Look up the product code below; some products have special return rules.
3. Create a return authorization (RMA) number in the ERP system and give it to the customer.
4. When the parcel arrives, the warehouse inspects it within 2 working days.
5. If the inspection passes, finance releases the refund.

## Products with special return rules

- ABC-10293 — Wireless headphones. Extended return window of 30 days. Must be returned with all ear tips and the charging cable.
- ABC-10294 — Custom engraved headphones. Not returnable, because the engraving is made to order. Only manufacturing defects are accepted under warranty.
- ABC-10295 — Headphone charging case. Standard 14-day return window.
- KTL-2210 — Electric kettle 1.7 L. Returnable only if the seal on the box is unbroken, for hygiene reasons.
- KTL-2211 — Electric kettle 1.0 L. Same rule as KTL-2210.
- DSK-7781 — Standing desk frame. Returns require pickup by our courier; the customer pays a 40 GEL pickup fee.

## Defective products

Defective products can be returned within 12 months under the warranty. The customer must describe the defect; the service team decides on repair, replacement or refund. For defective items the company pays all shipping costs.

## Refused returns

Returns are refused if the product shows signs of use, is missing parts, or the return window has passed. The customer is informed of the reason in writing and the product is sent back at the customer's expense.

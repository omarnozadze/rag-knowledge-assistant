# Northwind Supply — IT Security Policy

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across systems. Use the company password manager to generate and store them. Passwords are not rotated on a schedule; they must be changed immediately if there is any suspicion that they have been exposed.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the ERP system, the HR portal and the VPN. The approved method is an authenticator app; SMS codes are allowed only as a temporary fallback for up to 7 days.

## Phishing

Suspicious emails must not be opened or forwarded. Report them with the "Report phishing" button in the email client, or forward the message as an attachment to security@northwind.example. If you clicked a link or entered your password, contact the IT helpdesk immediately and change your password.

## Lost or stolen devices

A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 1 hour of noticing the loss, by phone at extension 555 or through a ticket of type SEC-INC. IT will lock and remotely wipe the device. The employee must also file a police report within 24 hours if the device was stolen.

## Software installation

Only software from the company software catalog may be installed. Requests for other software are submitted as a ticket of type IT-REQ and are reviewed by IT within 3 working days.

## Data classification

Documents are classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Confidential and Restricted documents must not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud accounts, and may only be shared outside the company with encryption.

# Northwind Supply — Leave Policy

## 1. Annual leave

Every full-time employee is entitled to 24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive annual leave in proportion to their contracted hours. New employees start accruing leave from their first working day, but may only take leave after completing the three-month probation period, unless their manager approves an exception in writing.

## 2. How to request annual leave

Leave requests are submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days before the first day of absence. The direct line manager approves or rejects the request within 2 working days. After approval, the HR department is notified automatically and the absence appears in the team calendar. Requests longer than 10 consecutive working days require additional approval from the department head.

## 3. Carry-over of unused days

Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over to the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used before 31 March; after that date they expire and are not paid out. Unused days above the carry-over limit are forfeited at year end unless the employee was prevented from taking leave by a documented business need.

## 4. Sick leave

An employee who is ill must inform their manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate must be uploaded to the HR portal. Paid sick leave is limited to 40 calendar days per year; beyond that, the statutory social insurance rules apply.

## 5. Parental leave

Parents are entitled to up to 183 calendar days of paid parental leave and an additional 547 days of unpaid parental leave. The employee should notify HR at least 30 days before the planned start date. Parental leave may be split between both parents.

## 6. Unpaid leave and special leave

Employees may request up to 15 days of unpaid leave per year for personal reasons. Three days of paid special leave are granted for an employee's own wedding, and three days for the death of a close family member.

# Northwind Supply — Travel and Expense Policy

## Booking business travel

Business trips must be approved by the line manager before any booking is made. Flights and hotels are booked through the travel desk, not personally. Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours.

## Daily allowance (per diem)

Employees on business trips receive a daily allowance to cover meals and local transport. The rates are 60 GEL per day within Georgia and 50 EUR per day abroad. On the day of departure and the day of return, half of the daily allowance is paid. No per diem is paid for days when meals are provided by the conference or the host.

## Submitting an expense claim

Expense claims are submitted with form FIN-207 in the expense system within 30 days after the trip or purchase. Each expense must have an itemized receipt; card slips alone are not accepted. Claims above 1,000 GEL require approval from the department head in addition to the line manager. Approved claims are reimbursed with the next monthly salary.

## Corporate cards

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than 6 times per year. The card may be used only for business expenses. Personal purchases on the card must be reported within 5 days and are deducted from salary. Lost corporate cards must be blocked through the bank's hotline and reported to finance the same day.

## Non-reimbursable expenses

Alcohol, fines, personal entertainment, minibar charges and upgrades to business class are not reimbursed. Taxi rides are reimbursed only when public transport is unavailable or the trip is between 23:00 and 06:00.
